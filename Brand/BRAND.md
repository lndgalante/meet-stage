# BetterDemos

## Positioning

**Stay in flow. Look polished.**

Functional line: **Share once. Switch freely.**

BetterDemos is a lightweight macOS app that removes the awkward parts of live
software demos. It gives presenters one clean stage for every app window, so
the audience sees intentional transitions while the presenter stays focused on
the story.

## Product description

BetterDemos removes the awkward parts of live software demos. Share the Demo
Stage once, then switch between app windows from a compact controller or global
shortcuts—without reopening your meeting's share picker or exposing your
desktop. Each window is confirmed live before it appears, and the stage adapts
to its dimensions for a clean, sharp result. You stay focused on the story;
BetterDemos handles the transitions.

### Short description

Share one clean stage and switch between Mac windows without interrupting your
demo.

### Launch-page copy

**Give better demos.**

Share one clean stage, switch between app windows instantly, and keep your
audience focused on the story.

## Audience

BetterDemos is for founders, product engineers, sales engineers, and anyone who
demos software live.

## Message pillars

- **Stay in flow.** Switch sources without stopping the demo.
- **Share intentionally.** Only the window you select appears on the Demo Stage.
- **Trust the handoff.** Live state appears only after a complete frame arrives.
- **Move quickly.** Keep important windows on stable global shortcuts.

## Voice

Calm, precise, and discreet. Use short sentences and direct verbs. Prefer the
product vocabulary **demo**, **Demo Stage**, **live**, **switch**, and **window**.
Avoid theatrical jokes, broadcasting jargon, and claims that imply a source is
live before it has been confirmed.

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | `#15191D` | App icon and presentation backdrop |
| Demo cyan | `#36D6FF` | Active window and primary brand accent |
| Paper | `#F4F7F8` | Supporting geometry and high-contrast text |

The icon's cyan center is the stable Demo Stage. The two side forms are app
windows waiting to be shown. Keep the mark wordless and use it on Night whenever
possible.

## Naming note

**BetterDemos** expresses the outcome rather than the mechanism: smoother flow
for the presenter and a more polished experience for the audience. The phrase
is also common category language, so complete trademark, company-name, and
domain clearance before a public commercial launch.
